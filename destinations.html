Popular Destinations

Sigiriya - The Lion Rock

Ticket Price: $30 USD (Foreign Tourists)

Opening Hours: 7:00 AM - 5:30 PM

Location: Central Province

Sigiriya, also known as Lion Rock, is one of Sri Lanka's most iconic historical sites and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. This ancient rock fortress rises 200 meters above the surrounding plains.

Highlights:

  • ✓ Ancient frescoes (Sigiriya Maidens)
  • ✓ The famous Mirror Wall
  • ✓ Beautifully landscaped water gardens
  • ✓ Panoramic views from the summit

Kandy - The Cultural Capital

Temple Entry: $10 USD

Opening Hours: 5:30 AM - 8:00 PM

Location: Central Province

Kandy is the last capital of the Sinhala kings and a UNESCO World Heritage Site, famous for the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic.

Highlights:

  • ✓ Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic
  • ✓ Kandy Lake
  • ✓ Royal Botanical Gardens
  • ✓ Traditional Cultural Shows

Ella - The Mountain Paradise

Best Time: January - March

Location: Uva Province

Ella is a charming small town in the Sri Lankan highlands, surrounded by mountains, waterfalls, and tea plantations.

Highlights:

  • ✓ Nine Arch Bridge
  • ✓ Ella Rock hiking
  • ✓ Ravana Falls
  • ✓ Tea factory tours

Galle Fort - Colonial Heritage

Beach Access: Free

Fort Open: 24 hours

Location: Southern Province

Galle Fort is a historic fortress built by Portuguese and Dutch colonial powers, now a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Highlights:

  • ✓ Dutch Fort architecture
  • ✓ Lighthouse and ramparts
  • ✓ Beaches and surfing
  • ✓ Colonial buildings and museums
